How a jewelry clip gets made

  1. Piece

    Start from a clear image of the jewelry.

  2. Scene

    Design a styled setting that suits the piece.

  3. Prompt

    Lock the piece's shape and metal into the video prompt, with the light and movement.

  4. Render

    Generate the short vertical clip with an AI video model.

  5. Finish

    Sharpen, color grade and add shine and sparkle to the stones.

  6. Publish

    Post the finished clip to social media.

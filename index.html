Jewelry Showcase

Short vertical social media designs for jewelry, showing each piece in a styled scene with light, shine and movement.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Piece

    One clean shot of the jewel.

  2. Scene

    Build its world: moss, marble, clouds.

  3. Prompt

  5. Finish

  6. Publish

    Vertical, looping and off to the feed.

1Cardz.news

A trading card game news channel fronted by two AI presenters, Zia and Luna. Each clip turns a verified headline into a short vertical news segment, filmed in one consistent arcade-library studio.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Research

    Hunt the freshest headline. Verify it twice.

  2. Script

    Cut the story to lines a host can land.

  3. Prompt

    Lock the host, studio and mic. Nothing drifts.

  4. Render

    Roll the clip. Check that host and studio hold.

  5. Finish

    Upscale it and add the captions.

  6. Publish

    One clip, three platforms.

Footy Footy

A cartoon series about Footy, a white mascot who rides a green turtle-bow boat through a mountain river valley of waterfalls, tunnels and cable cars. Small frogs, turtles, lizards, birds and flowers fill out the cast.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Idea

    One small adventure for Footy and the crew.

  2. World

    Pick a stop: waterfall, tunnel or cable car.

  3. Prompt

    Lock Footy, the turtle boat and the cartoon look.

  4. Render

    Shot by shot, the valley starts to move.

  5. Finish

    Cut it together and drop in the titles.

  6. Publish

    Send it downstream to the feeds.

Jewelry Showcase

Short vertical social media designs for jewelry, showing each piece in a styled scene with light, shine and movement.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Piece

    Start with one clean shot of the jewel. Every later step is built on its exact shape and detail.

  2. Scene

    Build a world that suits the piece, such as moss, marble or clouds, so it feels like more than a product shot.

  3. Prompt

    Pin down the prongs, metal and stones first, then describe the light and movement around them.

  4. Render

    AI turns the still into a short vertical clip, and the piece has to stay true to the original.

  5. Finish

    Sharpen and color grade the clip, then add shine and sparkle so the stones catch the eye.

  6. Publish

    The finished clip goes out as a vertical post, made to stop the scroll on social feeds.

1Cardz.news 1cardz.news ↗

A trading card game news channel fronted by two AI presenters, Zia and Luna. Each clip turns a verified headline into a short vertical news segment, filmed in one consistent arcade-library studio.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Research

    Hunt the freshest trading card headline and verify it against trusted sources before it goes any further.

  2. Script

    Cut the story into timed lines that Zia or Luna can deliver in a short vertical clip.

  3. Prompt

    Lock the host, arcade-library studio and news mic into the prompt, so every clip looks like the same show.

  4. Render

    Roll the clip, then check that the host and studio still match the rest of the series.

  5. Finish

    Upscale the render and add captions taken straight from the script, so it works with the sound off.

  6. Publish

    Schedule each clip to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, with new ones at 8 AM and 8 PM Hong Kong time.

Footy Footy footyfooty.com ↗

A cartoon series about Footy, a white mascot who rides a green turtle-bow boat through a mountain river valley of waterfalls, tunnels and cable cars. Small frogs, turtles, lizards, birds and flowers fill out the cast.

Tools
Claude + After Effects + Photoshop
Runs on
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok

  1. Idea

    Start with one small adventure for Footy and the crew, simple enough to follow in a short clip.

  2. World

    Pick a stop in the mountain river valley, such as a waterfall, tunnel or cable car, to set the scene.

  3. Prompt

    Lock Footy, the turtle boat and the cartoon look, so he stays the same character from clip to clip.

  4. Render

    Generate the story shot by shot with AI video models, until the valley starts to move.

  5. Finish

    Cut the shots together and add the titles, so they play as one story.

  6. Publish

    Send the finished clip to the social feeds and add it to footyfooty.com.
